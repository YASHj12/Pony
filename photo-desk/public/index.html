How it works

  1. Add photos. Drag a folder in, or paste screenshots straight from the clipboard.
  2. It looks at each one. Sharpness, exposure, contrast, colourfulness, framing and clutter — plus what the photo actually shows.
  3. You get the write-up. A category, a quality score with concrete fixes, five headlines written for your tone, a caption, tags, alt text and privacy flags.

On-device mode needs no account and no key: it measures the photo and writes from those measurements, and with “Smart on-device classification” on it also recognises what is in the picture. Adding a vision key upgrades the writing to a full multimodal model. Same interface either way.
